Teleop-Free Robot Learning

Pranay Gupta1 Deepanshu Rohilla1 Jiongli Zhu2 Parjanya Prashant2

1Argon Robotics    2UC San Diego

CoRL 2026 Late-Breaking Results

Abstract

Generalist robot policies have advanced rapidly in recent years, but they still need human teleoperation or intervention data for every new task. Simulation research has focused mostly on visual fidelity and keeps running into the infamous sim-to-real gap. We identify that the key gap in current simulation design is unmeasured, uncalibrated physics rather than visual fidelity. We frame simulation design as a multi-agent task: a builder agent, given calibrated physical values for every asset and measured distances, constructs the scene. Meanwhile an audit agent probes the physics for flaws and writes solvers for the task. We distill the trajectories from these solvers into π0.5 and show that the resulting policy generalizes to the real world without any real world demonstrations.
